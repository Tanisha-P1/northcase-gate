## Brand & Style
The design system channels an enterprise-grade digital engineering identity: forward-looking, high-velocity, rigorous, and technologically elite. It is tailored for Fortune 500 executives, engineering leaders, and digital transformation buyers seeking transformative architectural depth and business velocity.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Corporate Modern with High-Tech Energy**. It blends the disciplined structural clarity of high-end global IT consultancies with hyper-vibrant electric accents, deep cosmic navy/slate tones, and crisp architectural grid layouts. The interface evokes unyielding reliability, modern engineering intelligence, and clear digital execution without superfluous decorative noise.

## Colors
The palette leverages deep space canvas foundations punctuated by electric kinetic accents:
- **Primary (`#5F2CFF`):** A high-voltage digital violet reflecting synthetic intelligence, transformation, and modern engineering prestige. Used for primary CTAs, key focus states, and anchor links.
- **Secondary (`#02F576`):** An electric neon mint/green conveying speed, runtime health, innovation metrics, and positive telemetry. Used selectively as a high-impact accent, badge background, and indicator.
- **Neutral (`#0B0E14`):** The foundational obsidian canvas, grounding all experiences with deep contrast and low ocular fatigue across complex data workflows.
- **Semantic & Surface Accents:**
  - `slate-surface` (`#121824`): Structural surface layer for cards, sidebars, and elevated containers.
  - `slate-border` (`#1F293D`): Low-contrast architectural containment borders.
  - `pure-white` (`#FFFFFF`): High-legibility headline and high-priority data readouts.
  - `muted-gray` (`#94A3B8`): Contextual secondary metadata, supporting labels, and subheadings.

## Typography
Typography creates a strong architectural hierarchy pairing **Montserrat** for assertive, geometric headlines and navigation cues with **Inter** for utilitarian, dense, and effortlessly legible body copy.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in uppercase or tightly tracked sentence-case Montserrat with bold and extra-bold weights to convey executive strength and technical leadership.
- **Body & Data:** Powered by Inter to preserve legibility in high-density consulting tables, white papers, case studies, and enterprise service descriptions.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Rendered in Montserrat with medium to bold weights, often configured in uppercase with positive letter spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) to denote service pillars, status badges, and taxonomy tags.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a precise **12-column responsive fluid grid** with a maximum content container bound at 1440px to retain executive polish on widescreen monitors.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Desktop (>= 1200px)`: 12 columns, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, 2.5rem (`40px`) outer margins.
  - `Tablet (768px - 1199px)`: 8 columns, 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters, 2rem (`32px`) outer margins.
  - `Mobile (< 768px)`: 4 columns, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, 1.25rem (`20px`) outer margins.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Spacing operates strictly on a 4px/8px modular rhythm. Component internals rely on `space-sm` and `space-md`, while section separation and card layouts utilize `space-lg` through `space-xl` for breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is maintained using **tonal layered surfaces and crisp, luminous borders** rather than diffuse dark drop shadows:
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Pure neutral `#0B0E14`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** `#121824` layered with a 1px solid stroke of `#1F293D`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Overlays, Floating Menus):** `#182030` with a subtle diffuse ambient perimeter glow (`0 16px 36px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`).
- **Interactive Focus & Hover:** Elements gain depth via electric perimeter luminescence—such as a 1px border shift to `#5F2CFF` or an atmospheric mint shadow (`0 0 20px rgba(2, 245, 118, 0.15)`).

## Shapes
The design system adopts a **soft, precision-engineered geometric radius (`roundedness: 1`)**. 
- Base controls, buttons, text inputs, and metric badges use `0.25rem` (`4px`) corner radii.
- Content cards, modals, and container panels use `0.5rem` (`8px`) radii.
- Avoid hyper-rounded or fully pill shapes on standard interactive enterprise components; maintain rectilinear structure to communicate precision, architectural stability, and engineering rigor.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#5F2CFF`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Montserrat` semi-bold, 4px corner radius, padding `12px 24px`. On hover: `#4B20D4` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(95, 44, 255, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / Accent:** Background `#02F576`, text `#0B0E14`, font `Montserrat` bold. Used for key conversion moments and live demo actions.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Border `1px solid #1F293D`, text `#FFFFFF`, transparent background. On hover: border `#5F2CFF` and background `rgba(95, 44, 255, 0.08)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Capability Tags:** Compact height (`24px`), font `label-sm`, uppercase tracking. Neutral background `#121824` with `1px solid #1F293D` and text `#94A3B8`.
- **Active / Kinetic Badges:** Mint tint background (`rgba(2, 245, 118, 0.1)`), border `1px solid rgba(2, 245, 118, 0.4)`, text `#02F576`.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Structured containers with `#121824` fill and `1px solid #1F293D` perimeter.
- Top-accent option: a 2px hairline border using a linear gradient from `#5F2CFF` to `#02F576`.
- Interactive cards elevate on hover with a 2px upward translation (`transform: translateY(-2px)`) and border shift to `#5F2CFF`.

### Input Fields
- Dark canvas backgrounds (`#0B0E14`), border `1px solid #1F293D`, text `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `#64748B`.
- Focus state: border `1px solid #5F2CFF` with a 2px outer outline glow in `rgba(95, 44, 255, 0.25)`.
- Integrated monospace/code font compatibility for technical query fields or cloud resource parameters.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 16x16px bounds with 2px corner radius for checkboxes; circular for radio.
- Unchecked: border `1px solid #1F293D`, background `#121824`.
- Checked: background `#5F2CFF`, border `#5F2CFF`, crisp white glyph check.

### Key Consulting & Enterprise Modules
- **Metric Readout KPI Cards:** Large numeric statistics set in Montserrat Extra Bold (`display-hero-mobile`) in `#02F576` or `#FFFFFF`, complemented by an uppercase label-sm context header in `#94A3B8`.
- **Interactive Tech Stack Matrices:** High-density grid cells featuring monospace parameter values, slate dividing rules, and glowing status nodes.
